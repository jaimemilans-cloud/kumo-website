Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kumo hardware and privacy.

Is Kumo listening to me all the time?

No. Voice capture is strictly triggered when you interact with the device. Audio is processed on-device or on your local network, ensuring private conversations are never stored without authorization.

Does each wearable come with a charger?

Yes! Every Kumo Loop, Strap, or Pendant comes with a magnetic USB-C fast-charging cable in the box. The Kumo Dock is an optional bedside accessory that acts as a multi-device charging hub and ambient speaker.

Do I need a monthly subscription?

No subscriptions are required. Kumo leverages local open-source AI models (Whisper + Llama) so you own your hardware and data completely.
